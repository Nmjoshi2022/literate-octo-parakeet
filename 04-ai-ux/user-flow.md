## Entry point

**Signal type:** File / document uploaded

A product manager uploads customer interview transcripts, support ticket exports, or product feedback documents for analysis.

**What they see instantly**

Juno is analyzing your customer feedback and searching related product knowledge sources. Insights will appear shortly.

## The flow

1. 1. Parse uploaded transcript or feedback document
2. Retrieve related customer feedback and product knowledge using RAG
3. Identify recurring pain points, customer needs, and opportunities
4. Score opportunities based on impact, frequency, and strategic alignment
5. Generate prioritized recommendations with supporting evidence
2. Analyzing transcript.
Retrieving supporting evidence.
Identifying recurring pain points.

3. If transcript or feedback data is uploaded → RAG retrieval path
 
If user requests recommendations → Prioritization path

## AI moments

**Placement:** Inline & Embedded

Executive summary
 
Top pain points 
Priority opportunities ranked High / Medium / Low 
Supporting evidence snippets 
Recommended next actions 
Confidence score and source citations

Juno augments product managers by accelerating analysis and prioritization. It surfaces evidence-backed recommendations while keeping strategic decisions under human control.

## Fallbacks

**Kill switch**

Product managers can override any recommendation, adjust priorities, reject insights, or remove evidence sources before using the results.

**Training signal**

Accepted recommendations, rejected recommendations, manual priority adjustments, and edited summaries are logged to improve future prioritization quality.

**Fail-safe**

If relevant evidence cannot be retrieved or confidence falls below threshold, Juno displays "Insufficient evidence available" and requests additional context instead of generating unsupported recommendations.
