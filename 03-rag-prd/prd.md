## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Hybrid

Hybrid RAG combines semantic search with keyword matching.
 
Semantic retrieval identifies similar customer problems expressed differently, while keyword retrieval ensures direct matches for product names, features, and technical terminology.
 
This approach provides higher recall and accuracy than either method alone.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Juno should access:
 
- Customer interview transcripts (500+ transcripts)
- Support tickets and feature requests (10,000+ records)
- Product requirements documents (BRDs)
- Product roadmap documentation
- Customer satisfaction survey responses
- Internal product knowledge articles
 
This volume provides sufficient historical context to identify recurring pain points and prioritize opportunities based on evidence rather than isolated feedback.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** Every insight must include:
 
- Supporting evidence snippet
- Source document type
- Source reference or citation
- Confidence score
 
Users must be able to trace every recommendation back to the original source material.
- **Freshness:** Customer feedback and support ticket data should refresh daily.
 
Product roadmaps, BRDs, and product documentation should refresh whenever changes are published.
 
Maximum staleness: 24 hours.

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
