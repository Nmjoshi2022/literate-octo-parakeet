## Goal

Help Product Managers transform customer feedback into evidence-backed product priorities faster while maintaining human control over final roadmap decisions.

**Primary actor:** Human + Agent co-pilot

## Trigger

A customer interview transcript, support ticket export, or product feedback document is uploaded to Juno.

## Steps & tools

**Pattern:** ReAct (single-agent reason-act-observe loop)

| Step | Action | Tool / model | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parse the uploaded transcript or feedback document. | document_parser.parse(file): read-only | Juno can READ customer interview transcripts, support ticket data, product documentation, roadmap documents, and strategy knowledge bases.   Juno can WRITE recommendation history, approved prioritization results, and user feedback signals.   Juno CANNOT modify roadmaps, release plans, customer records, support tickets, or business systems without Product Manager approval. |
| 2 | Retrieve related evidence from customer feedback and product knowledge sources. | rag_retrieval.search(query): read-only |  |
| 3 | Identify recurring pain points and opportunities. | prioritization_engine.score(opportunities): read-only |  |
| 4 | Score opportunities based on impact, frequency, and strategic alignment. | summary_generator.create(report): read-only |  |
| 5 | Generate prioritized recommendations with supporting evidence. | recommendation_history.save(decision): write |  |
| 6 | Present results to the Product Manager for review. | _ |  |

**Schemas**

document_parser -> {text, metadata, source}
 
rag_retrieval -> {chunks:[{text, source, score}]}
 
prioritization_engine -> {opportunities:[{title, priority, impactScore, rationale}]}
 
summary_generator -> {executiveSummary, recommendations, nextActions}
 
recommendation_history -> {status, recommendationId}

**Memory (in or out of scope)**

- **Episodic:** Current analysis session, uploaded files, generated insights, confidence scores, and recommendation drafts.
- **Semantic:** Product strategy documents, prioritization frameworks, accepted recommendations, and product management best practices.
- **Working:** Current transcript, retrieved evidence chunks, ranking scores, and intermediate reasoning steps.
- **External:** Customer interview repository (read)
Support ticket database (read)
Product documentation repository (read)
Strategy knowledge base (read)
Recommendation history database (read/write)

## Human-in-the-loop

The Product Manager reviews all recommendations before action is taken. Human review is required when confidence is below 80%, evidence is insufficient, or strategic prioritization decisions affect the roadmap.

## Success & failure

- **Done when:** Success: Recommendations and evidence generated successfully.
Failure: Required document cannot be processed or evidence cannot be retrieved.
Escalation: Confidence score below 80% or conflicting evidence exists.
Timeout: Analysis exceeds system processing limits.
- **Fails safe when:** Juno can READ customer interview transcripts, support ticket data, product documentation, roadmap documents, and strategy knowledge bases.
 
Juno can WRITE recommendation history, approved prioritization results, and user feedback signals.
 
Juno CANNOT modify roadmaps, release plans, customer records, support tickets, or business systems without Product Manager approval.
